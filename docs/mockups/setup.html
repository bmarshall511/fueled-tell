Host setup, reimagined as an app

✦ Move your cursor over any screen below: the glow follows you and cards catch the light.

Setup becomes a studio instead of a long form. Who's playing is the main stage. Everything else (settings, readiness, a live preview of the shared screen, and the one big action) sits in a sticky side panel. On a phone the same pieces stack, and the action stays pinned at the bottom.

What changes

From form to studio

People, not rows

Each person is a card with their color avatar and a story preview. Problems show on the card itself, and you edit in place.

A quick-add composer

Name, story, Enter. Paste a whole list into either field and it opens the paste sheet automatically.

A live preview

A mini shared screen shows what everyone will see, so the settings feel concrete.

One action, always visible

A readiness meter ("6 of 8 ready") and a big "Open lobby" pill. Nothing to scroll down to.

A calmer privacy note

A small pill in the top bar ("Private · don't share your screen yet") replaces the long yellow banner.

Reacts to your cursor

The glow trails the pointer, the blobs shift in parallax, and glass cards and buttons light up under the cursor. Nothing moves position. Off on touch devices and with reduced motion.

Livelier buttons

Hover: a light sweep and a rotating gradient ring. Press: a darker fill with a ripple. Buttons never move. Busy: a spinner. Done: a gradient check. Option toggles slide a pill, and switches spring.

A friendlier empty state

Three big choices to start (Paste a list, Add one by one, Players add their own), plus "Try sample stories".
