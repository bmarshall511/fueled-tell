Tell, retheme: one Fueled system

The look of fueled.com applied to every screen of the game, so the shared screen and the phones look like one product. Nothing in the app has changed yet. If you approve this, it becomes tokens and shared components, and every screen inherits them.

What changes

From chamfered blocks to soft, glassy, rounded

Shape

  • Pill buttons, chips and tags
  • 32px-radius cards, 24px panels, 18px fields
  • The chamfered corners go away everywhere

Surface

  • Dark translucent "glass" with a hairline border and backdrop blur
  • The white story card stays as the hero, now rounded with a soft violet glow

Motion and colour

  • A slow-drifting gradient glow (Solar → Nebula → lilac) behind every screen
  • Vivid on landing, lobby and finale; calm and low during rounds
  • Static when reduced motion is on

One system

  • Host and phone share the same buttons, chips, fields, cards and backdrop
  • The host still scales everything up for screen share
  • The 3D deck cards get rounded corners to match

Components

The kit

Primary actions are white pills with black text: the highest contrast you can get, and fueled.com's modern feel without putting small text on Nebula. Secondary actions are glass pills.
